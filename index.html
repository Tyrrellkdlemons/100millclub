Signing you in automatically is not possible.

The charts on this page are TradingView's public embeds. They load inside a sandboxed frame on a different domain, so they cannot see that you are signed in to tradingview.com — browsers block that, and TradingView offers no way to pass a session into an embed. Any site claiming otherwise is not doing what it says.

What does work is a handoff: the TradingView button opens the symbol and timeframe you are looking at on tradingview.com itself, where you already are signed in. You get your own plan there — real-time entitled data, your layouts, indicators and alerts.

How to use the terminal

1 · Pick something to trade

Click any row in the Watchlist on the left. Use the tabs at the top (All, Stocks, Crypto, Forex, Indices) to narrow the list, or type in the search box to find a symbol or company name instantly.

2 · Live chart or simulated chart

The Live toggle shows the real TradingView chart with genuine market data and its own drawing and indicator toolbars. Simulated switches to the built-in engine, which is what powers this dashboard's own indicators, drawings and backtests — and it keeps working with no internet connection.

3 · Read the chart

Green bars closed up, red bars closed down. The timeframe buttons (1m → 1w) change how much time each bar covers. Indicators adds average lines and a momentum panel. Drawing tools lets you mark a trend line or a price level. Fullscreen makes the chart fill the screen.

4 · Place a practice order

On the right, choose BUY or SELL, set how many units, then optionally add a stop loss (your safety exit) and a take profit (your target exit). Place the order and it appears under Open positions with live profit and loss. Stops and targets fire automatically as prices move.

5 · Test a strategy before risking anything

Switch to Test a strategy, pick a rule set and a date range, then Run backtest. You get total return, win rate, number of trades, Sharpe ratio, worst drawdown, the final balance, an equity curve against buy & hold, and a full trade log.

6 · The bottom section

Vlogs holds video drops — each card has share buttons for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X. Technicals, News, Screener, Heatmap and Calendar are live TradingView panels with real market data. Collapse the whole section with the caret, or hide it with the Vlogs button in the top bar.

7 · Using your own TradingView account

The charts and market panels built into this page are TradingView's public embeds. They always load anonymously — they run in a sandboxed frame that cannot read your tradingview.com login, so they cannot show your subscription's real-time feeds no matter who is signed in. That is a TradingView platform rule, not a setting here.

To see something on your own plan, use the blue TradingView button in the chart toolbar (or in the panel header). It opens the exact symbol and timeframe you are looking at on tradingview.com. If you are already signed in there, it loads with everything you pay for — real-time entitled data, your saved layouts, your indicators and your alerts. If you are not signed in, you simply get the normal free view.

This is also why the index charts here use TradingView's own index feeds rather than the exchange tickers: the exchange versions need a paid data entitlement and refuse to load in an embed. The TradingView button still sends you to the entitled ticker, so subscribers get the real feed.

8 · Make it yours

Click the crest in the top-left corner to upload your own logo — it is remembered on this device.
