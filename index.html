How to use the terminal

1 · Pick something to trade

Click any row in the Watchlist on the left. Use the tabs up top (All, Stocks, Crypto, Forex, Indices) to narrow the list, or type in the search box to find a symbol fast.

2 · Read the chart

Green bars closed up, red bars closed down. The timeframe buttons (1m → 1w) change how much time each bar covers. Indicators adds average lines and a momentum panel. Drawing tools lets you mark a trend line or a price level. Fullscreen makes the chart fill the screen.

3 · Place a practice order

On the right, choose BUY or SELL, set how many units, then optionally add a stop loss (your safety exit) and a take profit (your target exit). Hit place and the order shows up under Open positions with live profit and loss.

4 · Test a strategy before risking anything

Switch to Test a strategy, pick a rule set and a date range, then Run backtest. You get total return, win rate, number of trades, Sharpe ratio, worst drawdown, the final balance, an equity curve and a trade log.

5 · Vlogs

The row under the chart holds video drops. Each card has share buttons for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X — click one to open a pre-filled share link, or copy the link to post anywhere.
